## Settings

- Protocol T per cell: total of 2000, 20000 evaluations per repetition, runs of at most the total, no run started with fewer than 100 remaining; root seed 3
- Requested repetitions per cell: 20
- Incumbents: bite, de, cma, crfmnes, da, de_cma, jso_cma; candidate: catcmawm (`cmaes.CatCMAwM`, Python reference implementation, default parameters)
- Cells (problem, total) expected: 18; complete: 18
- Tests: two-sided Mann-Whitney U (`scipy.stats.mannwhitneyu`, default method) against the best incumbent of the cell (lowest median, ties by lower mean); a NaN p-value (all values identical) counts as 1; Holm correction over the candidate's complete cells; significant when the adjusted p-value is at most 0.05
- Ranks: by median among the 8 solvers in each complete cell (average ranks for ties), averaged over the complete cells
- Resampling for G2: 10,000 resamples (NumPy `default_rng(3)`); in each, the repetitions of every cell and solver are drawn with replacement, all mean ranks are recomputed, and the resample counts when the candidate's mean rank is strictly below every incumbent's

## Limits

- The union holds the six round-3 problems (DEVIATIONS.md, round 3, entry 2) and the round-3b batch (ROUND3B.md section 2). Problems with an unordered categorical coordinate: field-service routing and the job shop of round 3 and the three round-3b problems.
- Binary switches are typed integer, as declared, although the `cmaes` library recommends categorical for them (DEVIATIONS.md, round 3, entry 3).
- The candidate is the authors' Python reference implementation; it is compared by objective evaluations only, never by time. Its seed is the low 32 bits of the shared run seed (entry 4).
- 20 repetitions per cell; small effects can stay undetected and Holm correction is conservative.
- Published defaults only; no solver was tuned.
- The decision follows the declared rules mechanically; this report draws no other conclusion.
